# 0210 — Regional journeys on the home board

The existing board mixes general gathering with landmark visits. Two new outings
give mountain exploration and lakeshore life distinct routes and rewards without
adding a second quest system.

Append expedition IDs 9 and 10. The mountain outing visits the spring and gathers
stone and ore from authoritative mountain patches. The lakeshore outing visits
the large lake, gathers nearby reeds and catches fish whose float is at the lake.
Small landward arrival margins allow progress from safe trails and banks.

Optional region tags on gather and catch events restrict the new objectives while
unscoped old objectives still accept both tagged and untagged actions. Positions
and patch identities are evaluated on the server. Inventory transfers do not
produce gathering events. Existing three-objective saves and packets are reused.

Both trips rotate from the tent tier; Bonehollow remains pinned. Rewards reuse
existing clothing and food. The lakeshore hint explicitly warns about winter ice.
There is no timer, new currency, asset or additional database state.
