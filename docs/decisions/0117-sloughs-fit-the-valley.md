# 0117 · Sloughs fit the valley

## Context

Chris found slough water projecting off hillsides even after the water was sloped. Chris approved resizing or moving unsuitable pools and requested varied larger rocks around them.

## Decision

- Reduce the first three pools and move them to gentler reaches. Bring all six pools closer to the river so their footprints fit its valley. Keep the broad, open connections and larger pools on the lower bends.
- Shape supported shallow bowls and dry rims, blending back into the surrounding ground over eight metres. The previous shaping only cut ground, leaving water above falling hillsides. Rims now support both water and ice; the original river channel stays open at each mouth.
- Use the nearest slough's bowl when banks approach one another, so an outer bank cannot fill another pool.
- Give each slough a different deterministic plant layout and four to seven larger scenery rocks, varying size, proportions and rotation. Reuse the existing stone geometry and material. Keep the mouths clear and embed rock bases across their footprints, rejecting overly steep placements. Place small shore stones on actual ground height too.

## Consequences

Sloughs fit the land without tall artificial platforms, retaining shared terrain and ice collision on client and server. Regression checks cover exposed rims and the underlying valley across four world seeds, alongside the existing shallow-floor and open-mouth checks. Larger rocks remain scenery, consistent with the existing river stones.
