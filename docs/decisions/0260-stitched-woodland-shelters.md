# Stitched woodland shelters

Chris's explicit non-Blender housing authorization also covers tents and teepees.
Retain the existing shaped canvas meshes and timber silhouettes. Add narrow
reinforcement tape and individual stitches along pinned panel edges, broad hems,
wooden entrance toggles and a tucked-in groundsheet. Keep thread and tape on the
actual shared edges rather than floating a pattern over the curved panel centers.

Both authored and fallback cloth use the same muted sage (tent) or warm linen
(teepee) palette over the existing woven texture. The existing entrance lantern
now gives a small, daylight-aware pooled light. It adds no gameplay warmth or
cooking interaction. Its own emissive material and glow are disposed with the
shelter, and stitched geometry merges into the existing material batches.

Footprints, poles, door interaction and construction rules remain unchanged.
No new downloadable model or texture, and no changes to original Blender source.
Review front, rear and night views of both shelter tiers in the gallery.
