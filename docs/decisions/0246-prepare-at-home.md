# Prepare at home, follow the next trail

The existing home board already saves one outing, and the game already supports cooking, equipment, warmth, private storage and backpack capacity. Connect those systems before adding another progression currency.

An unfinished chosen outing now includes a live preparation guide in C → Expeditions. Objectives determine whether an axe, fishing rod or combat equipment is useful; refined tools count, and rods/torches/shovels do not satisfy combat advice. Worn equipment supplies protection and insulation. Winter uses the existing 60 insulation requirement, or 80 in a blizzard. The guide recommends two food servings, two free slots and starting with at least 80% of maximum health and 60% food. These are advice, never departure or reward gates.

An optional meal suggestion links to the existing recipes and their actual benefits. Food, equipment and backpack shortcuts use the existing panel transitions; the active server outing remains saved. Readiness is derived from the current inventory, worn gear, health and weather, without stored checkmarks or new messages. Completed outings hide preparation so claiming rewards stays prominent.

No auto-eating, auto-equipping, extra buffs, new recipes or automatic storage transfers are introduced. Food retains its existing equip/eat behavior; special meals retain their single active benefit. The gallery exercises actual meal crafting in an isolated journal preview, and a game browser check covers all three shortcuts.
