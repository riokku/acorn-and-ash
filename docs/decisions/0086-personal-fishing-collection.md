# 0086 — A gentle rare catch and a personal fishing journal

Ordinary pond catches keep their existing one-click flow. A hooked golden carp asks for two steady pulls in separate two-second cycles, with a broad visible window, extra input grace, and room for two mistakes. The challenge lasts at most eight seconds of acknowledged player inputs. Its clock starts when the browser acknowledges the new phase; queued bite inputs cannot spend that time. Fresh acknowledged responses refresh the twenty-second quiet-browser fallback, so slow rendering cannot spend the input-clock challenge. Walking away or a genuinely quiet browser releases the fish. The server owns every catch and uses seeded species and size rolls.

Every landed fish records species, count and best size for this character in this world. A full pack releases the fish but still records the catch and expedition fishing progress. Records and inventory save together; collection and challenge packets go only to the angler. Reconnecting restores the collection but cancels unfinished casts. Old saves begin with an empty collection.

Five catches unlock a carved fish display, and recording all three species unlocks the golden collection display. Both are stat-free original procedural props, placeable indoors and in the owner's outdoor building area; costs and collision checks still apply. Recipe eligibility is derived from saved catches and validated by the server. An equipped rod aims at the ground or water under the cursor, without tree/creature attack targeting intercepting its cast. Existing wire identifiers and eight input-button bits are preserved; the rare phase reuses the bite acknowledgement transition rather than adding a ninth bit.
