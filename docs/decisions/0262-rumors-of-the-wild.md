# Rumors of the Wild

Connect the existing home board, woodland landmarks and buried-supplies art with
three optional clue-led outings. Append expedition IDs 12–14, preserving every
old definition, saved progress shape and the 21-byte expedition packet. The
first offer alternates rumors and ordinary outings; the other slots remain
Sunreach and Bonehollow. Three successive rumor cycles cover all three stories.

Each personal trail has two deliberate landmark reads and a shovel-gated final
bundle. The server checks ordered progress, reach, elevation and guards at the
final site. Shared landmark positions and existing art avoid changing terrain,
old scenery IDs, collision or player homes. The personal mound appears only
when its final clue is known; another player cannot empty it.

World seed, cycle and rumor ID choose one of three story/reward outcomes, stable
across logout. Reveal it at completion and use the same shared choice for the
board preview and authoritative claim. Existing home-only atomic claims keep a
full pack from losing the find and prevent duplicate payouts. No timer, daily
reset or new currency. Progress uses existing expedition persistence. Ordinary
outings and already-active saves retain their behavior.
