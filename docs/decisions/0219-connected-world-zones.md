# Connected world zones: Sunreach High Plains

The world was one forest/mountain region. Sunreach now occupies the northwestern
frontier, reached by walking through Windbreak Pass. Both named zones share the
same simulation, calendar, character, inventory, housing and exploration map;
crossing the boundary requires no transfer or loading screen. A typed zone table
records names, climate and intended danger for future regions.

Shared deterministic terrain blends a dry plateau, dune pockets and Crown Mesa
into the existing hills. The authored pass has gentle grades and a clear walking
corridor. Tree thinning and path/den clearance happen after scenery numbering;
recorded forest IDs outside the new region keep their positions. Original scenery
heights continue to determine seeded choices. New boulders use IDs 46000–46089;
existing world bounds and exploration bytes do not change.

Jackrabbit/coyote dens append IDs 1101–1108. Coyotes have readable attacks, a
40-metre pursuit leash and can hunt jackrabbits when not fighting a player. Both
new kinds have collision bodies. Their initial figures reuse existing rabbit
and fox art at adjusted scales; dedicated creature models require a later
Blender art session. No new asset files or textures are introduced.

Dry grass, sandy ground, explored-map labels and a minimap zone label distinguish
the region. Plains visibility expands gradually; blizzards and underground fog
retain their limits. The gallery offers overview, dunes, pass and wildlife views.
