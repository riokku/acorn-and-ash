# Cabin craftsmanship without Blender

Chris explicitly authorized a non-Blender housing polish pass on 2026-10-27.
Keep the existing authored cedar roof and joinery, adding modest procedural
finishing pieces using the game's existing painted materials. This exception is
scoped to this housing pass; it does not change the general art workflow.

Individual chamfered footing stones, divided side/rear windows, attic louvers,
a doorway drip cap and porch post collars make the houses read as constructed
from real materials at walking distance. Side and rear panes share the existing
fireplace-controlled glass, so an exhausted fire cannot leave new windows lit.
Foundation pieces use the established hillside grounding; door interaction,
footprints, collision, storage and construction costs stay unchanged.

Parts merge by material, own their geometry and dispose with the cabin. There
are no new texture downloads or asset licensing changes. Review front, rear,
three-quarter and night views in the gallery before publishing.
