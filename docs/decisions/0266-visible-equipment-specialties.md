# Know your kit

Make the existing equipment identities easy to discover and compare. The
character screen has a compact, keyboard-accessible Your kit section listing
specialties that are actually active. Clothing must occupy a valid worn slot;
weapons must be in the active hand. Items in the pack or off hand cannot claim
their active-weapon perks. Holding food also removes an active-weapon specialty.

Keep name, benefit and acquisition/limitation text together in shared equipment
trait data. Existing item descriptions retain their wording, including when an
admin edits flavor text. No numbers, attack timings or loot rules change.

Inventory comparisons explain both gained and surrendered specialties. Weapon
comparisons preview the main-hand equip action, matching an ordinary item click,
rather than an available off-hand slot. Right-clicking and explicit drag targets
keep their existing slot behavior. Expand the kit only when requested, bound its
scrolling content, and keep the full character panel within short viewports.
