# Stories brought home

The game already has earned guardian, sentinel, Bonehollow and Hollow King trophies, Sunreach and expedition pennants, and two fishing displays. Players should understand those rewards and see the adventures reflected at home before another collection or currency is added.

C → Trophies presents eight keepsake stories, their actual acquisition routes, current recipe/material readiness, and a count of distinct kinds currently placed. C → Home links to the collection. Outdoor placement uses the existing build action and authoritative requirements; indoor decoration stays on B. Own outdoor props and own-home indoor decorations drive the collection, including restored pieces. Carried items alone do not count. Reclaiming removes a display from the current count; this is not a permanent achievement history.

Hovering an outdoor keepsake within five metres reads its story and distinguishes your piece from a neighbor's. Picking runs on the existing 80 ms cadence, considers at most eight nearby trophies and uses the same hidden-mesh and scenery occlusion checks as pantry interaction. Owner identities stay private under the established built-prop protocol. There is no claim about the owner's name, earning date or specific catch size.

Existing models, recipes, rewards, collision and reclaim rules are reused unchanged. No new assets, persistent state, stat bonuses or network layouts are needed. The gallery compares the previous recipe list with the collection and demonstrates spending a real trophy in its isolated preview.
