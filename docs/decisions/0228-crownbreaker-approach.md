# 0228 — The King’s Road

## Context

Crownbreaker Hold needed a deliberate approach from Bonehollow, with escalating encounters and room to prepare before engaging the Hollow King.

## Decision

A winding worn road links Bonehollow’s southern edge to the Last Muster, 25 metres from the King and outside his aggression range. Its gentle tread interpolates the two existing plateaus, with a small rise and a broad final apron. Scenery is filtered by its existing footprint rather than renumbered. Ground appearance and movement use the same route.

Two fixed ruined watchposts introduce a rogue/minion scouting pair and a sentinel/warrior/mage guard. They have short leashes, normal ten-minute encounter rest and independent bounded slots, so random forest encounters cannot suppress the approach or the boss. Players can retreat and take other routes; there is no mandatory kill gate. Existing masonry, cairns, pennants and skeleton models are reused without new assets. Short location cues tell the story along the path; the boss health bar begins inside the arena rather than covering the approach.

Only an encounter-clear message at the King’s exact authored site triggers his victory. Clearing a nearby watchpost must not be mistaken for defeating the boss.

## Consequences

Only the authored corridor changes terrain and scenery. Existing tree IDs, unrelated terrain and water shaping remain stable. The gallery provides road and ground-level muster viewpoints at `?gallery=crownbreaker`; `&before` hides the added watchposts and their guards to compare the scene dressing. Group balance still needs live multiplayer feedback.
