# Sticks from felled trees

Chopping previously left logs but no loose branches. Each tree species now defines a small stick yield alongside its log yield: two for western redcedar and alder, three for Douglas-fir, Sitka spruce and maple.

Scatter individual stick piles beside the fallen trunk in the final cutter's fall direction. Use the existing clear-drop search to keep them accessible and the same delayed landing time, persistence, expiry and multiplayer pickup rules as fallen logs. Restoring a felled tree does not recreate rewards; saved piles restore separately. Existing stick art and pickup interactions are reused.

Log yields and chopping timing stay the same. Full packs still fell trees and leave both rewards on the ground. Trees burned by wildfire retain their existing behavior.
