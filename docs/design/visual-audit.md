# Visual audit: cabin → forest → skeleton → home

Baseline source: `de60bcab67cd9acb4e1088ae4ee163cb2d03cc3f` (main). This audit distinguishes source inspection, historical repository images and fresh rendered evidence. Historical images identify questions; they do not prove the current build has the same issue. Nothing below certifies motion, sound quality or performance without a corresponding observed run.

## First focused improvement

**P1: keep the camera out of evergreen foliage.** A controlled review of the Cedar trail showed a solid foreground branch wedge hiding the nearby path. Existing camera blockers represented tree trunks but omitted the broader canopy. Adding camera-only canopy proxies gives a clearly visible improvement at the reproduced trail pose while preserving the rendered trees, player collision and orbit controls.

This is a bounded camera fix. It does not guarantee an unobstructed view through every surrounding branch: a second walking position retains a large off-center crown. That remaining obstruction is P2 follow-up, not a claimed result of this change.

The earlier daylight color trial was too subtle to establish a useful player-facing improvement. Further lighting changes are deferred and excluded from this PR. Preserve established night visibility, material colors, fog, fire behavior and weather transitions.

## Evidence and review limits

Fresh baseline galleries cover cabin day/night, trees, furnished home and skeleton lineup. They establish coherent timber construction, distinct evergreen/broadleaf silhouettes, readable ivory/burgundy/teal skeleton materials and an existing strong fire accent. They do not establish live encounter timing or navigation. Historical images under `docs/previews/` supplied the initial questions in the table, not matched proof of a current defect.

The generic gallery applies season after its daylight update, so its summer materials appear under the untinted base light rig. It is not proof of summer gameplay lighting. The controlled game-camera fixture applies the game's seasonal lighting and uses actual game assets and the follow camera.

The default player appears amber/gold. Source inspection confirms the local character uses the supplied identity independently of the remote-player roster and applies the selected tint across its materials. This appearance is not evidence of a skin-lighting defect or a missing model. No claim covers all six cosmetic bodies.

All route captures below are **controlled game-camera reviews using a local simulation fixture**, not a live online playthrough. Player position and view are matched within each pair; world geometry, season, time and weather are held fixed. Particle and idle-animation phases can differ. No claim is made about online persistence, sound quality, a completed skeleton encounter or overall performance from these stills.

## Camera evidence and acceptance

The initial Cedar trail pose shows a clear before/after difference:

| Before                                                     | After                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Trail before](../previews/canopy-camera/trail-before.png) | [Trail after](../previews/canopy-camera/trail-after.png) |

Before, an opaque foreground wedge hides much of the near ground. After, the path and ground around the player are visible. The trees still frame the route and the full character remains at a sensible third-person distance. This is the accepted player-facing improvement.

A separate controlled input run moved the player with W approximately 22.4 metres, from (48, 16) to (60.78, 34.38). The settled baseline still showed a broad crown covering the right side of the view. The candidate at the same destination is a remaining-gap control:

| Before                                                                  | After                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Walking destination before](../previews/canopy-camera/walk-before.png) | [Walking destination after](../previews/canopy-camera/walk-after.png) |

That off-center crown remains substantially unchanged. The candidate does not solve all foreground branch occlusion; it prevents camera entry into canopy geometry. Do not move trees or stage a different view to conceal this limitation. A broader view-clearance policy would need a separate assessment of camera pull-in and stability.

**Source review:** the change adds canopy proxies to the existing merged camera blocker meshes, using loaded foliage bounds when available and the established tree dimensions as a fallback. It retains trunk blockers and camera easing. Rendered assets, server walking collision, tree placement, controls, lighting, weather and progression are unchanged. No new models, textures or lights are introduced.

**Acceptance:** approved for the narrow canopy-entry fix. Final matched day, night and rain views remove the foreground wedge while retaining full-character framing. Night keeps its established dark visibility floor, and the rainy view retains a readable route. The walking-destination control retains the documented off-center crown, with no demonstrated improvement there. This is not winter/blizzard or full-route validation. Automated validation is recorded with the PR; these images do not substitute for it.
